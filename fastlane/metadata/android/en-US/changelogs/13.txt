Fixes messages from one contact arriving in the notification but never appearing in the conversation. The app no longer tries to match phone numbers itself or stitch conversations together, and no longer stops searching after a fixed number of records; it asks Android which conversation a message belongs to and reads that conversation directly, the way the system messaging app does. The conversation list now comes from Android's own conversation index instead of being assembled by trawling every message. Adds a conversation diagnostic under press and hold, so if anything like this happens again you can send us a report that says exactly where the mismatch is.
