Fixes received messages that only appeared in the notification and never in the conversation, which happened when Android filed one person under two conversation ids because their number arrived in a different format from the one you saved. Conversations are now matched on the number itself and split threads are shown joined back up. Incoming calls no longer play the message sound before the ringtone. The screen always comes back after you move the phone from your ear, including on phones with virtual proximity sensing, and there is a new switch under Settings, Calls, to turn screen blanking off. Conversations and titles now show names from your phone's contacts as well as the vault. Messages that fail to send are marked failed instead of being recorded as sent. Replies sent from a notification now appear in the conversation and long ones are split properly. File Scan can now upload a file to VirusTotal when VirusTotal has never seen it, one file at a time and only when you say so, and you can scan a single app instead of sweeping them all. The geo spoofer now covers network location as well as GPS.
