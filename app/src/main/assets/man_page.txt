DresSecureComms 1.8.0

DresSecureComms is a FOSS, de-Googled, all-in-one secure communications app.

Features:
- Messages: a private, offline SMS client. Send and receive normal texts, or flip on
  encryption per message. Encrypted messages are AES-256-GCM and only work between two
  people who both use this app and share the same key. Keys are set per contact from the
  conversation menu, so every person can have their own code, and the shared key in
  Settings still covers anyone you have not set one for. Also comes with photo sharing,
  group creation, search, and contact suggestions when you start a new message.
- Calls: dial any number or call a saved contact. Calls use your normal carrier line. The
  call log is searchable, the screen turns off when you hold the phone to your ear, and you
  can block or unblock any number straight from the log.
- Spam Shield: screens incoming calls before your phone rings. Rejects premium rate
  numbers, silences short codes, flags callers faking the first digits of your own number,
  and will silence anyone not in your contacts or reject withheld numbers if you want it
  stricter. Saved contacts always ring through. Everything it blocks is listed in Settings
  so you can undo it.
- Contacts: an encrypted local vault. Add contacts by hand or import them from the device.
  Everything is stored encrypted at rest using the Android Keystore.
- Threat Scan: check a URL against VirusTotal using your own free API key.
- File Scan: check a single file, or every app on your device, against VirusTotal. The file
  never leaves your phone. Only its SHA-256 fingerprint is sent, and that is checked against
  seventy or more antivirus engines at once. A free API key allows four lookups a minute, so
  a full sweep of your apps is paced accordingly and can be stopped at any point.
- Metadata Wipe: strip GPS and EXIF metadata from a photo before you share it.
- Geo Spoofer: set a mock GPS location, or have the app pick a random one. Enable this app
  as the mock location app under Developer options first.
- App Lock: biometric or device PIN protection.
- Block Screenshots: stop screenshots and screen recording inside the app.

Allowing SMS (one-time, required):
Because this app is installed from outside the Play Store, Android may block its SMS access
under "Restricted settings". If SMS does not work, do this once:
  1. Open Android Settings, then Apps, then DresSecureComms.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Allow restricted settings".
  4. Reopen DresSecureComms and grant the SMS permission when asked.

Setting up Spam Shield:
Open Settings inside the app, then Default apps, and set DresSecureComms as your caller ID
and spam app. The screening rules are under Call screening in the same Settings screen.

How it works:
- SMS and calls work fully offline on your carrier line. No Google services, no trackers.
- Internet is only used by Threat Scan and File Scan, and both talk to VirusTotal and
  nothing else.
- For encrypted SMS, agree the key with your contact by hand, in person or over another
  channel. Never send a key by plain text. Only this app can read the result.
- Encryption covers SMS text only. Pictures and group messages (MMS) are always sent
  unencrypted, the same as any normal messaging app, so they arrive readable on any phone.
- Spam screening runs entirely on the device. No number is ever sent anywhere to be checked.

Coming soon:

  connection or an API key.
- Stronger encryption across more of the app, not just messages and the contact vault.
- Several smaller refinements throughout.

You may experience some issues with SMS and Calling due to the encryption and security
unless you make it your default app.

DresSecureComms is a core part of DresOS, the Android defensive security system. It brings
private messaging, calling, contacts, threat scanning, spam screening, and location
protection together in one place so much of the system's day-to-day protection runs through
this single app. You can read more about the wider project from the About screen.
